# Code of Conduct

CardLab welcomes people who participate in good faith, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or viewpoint. Be direct about technical disagreements and respectful toward the people
involved.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing evidence, assumptions, and reproducibility without personal attacks;
- giving credit for prior work and clearly marking borrowed ideas;
- making space for questions and correcting mistakes without humiliation;
- disclosing conflicts of interest and relevant experimental limitations;
- respecting privacy and avoiding the publication of credentials or personal data.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, and deliberate falsification of results are not acceptable.

Report conduct or safety concerns through the private contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Project
maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a participant when
necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with
attention to the safety of the reporter.
